## Description
Comprehensive, evidence-based commercial census of Kafr El-Sheikh Governorate: identify, classify, count and map discoverable businesses across all markaz, cities and villages, estimate market size and revenue benchmarks, and rank investment gaps/opportunities. Delivered in persistent batches with a master database, not a one-off summary.
